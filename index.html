HOW TO PLAY

  • SLICE fruit for score + coins. Slice WELL — near the top of the arc (GREAT / PERFECT) — to build your TIFFED OFF meter.
  • Coins buy the attack. TIFFED OFF powers it. At 40% your attacks are HEATED, 70% FUMING, 100% a signature TIFFED OFF version. Attacking vents the meter.
  • Keep it clean: CLEAN combos (every cut GREAT+) and chained PERFECTs fill it faster. Misses, bombs, fakes and idling drain it.
  • Bombs fall on their own too. A bomb costs a heart and score — more for each heart you lose.
  • Duds have a green fuse and a green ripple: TAP them for a bonus. Swipe one and it fizzles in your face.
  • Attacks: BOMBS (cheap — but spamming them gets pricier), a disguised FAKE, a fruit RUSH, or a GUST. Watch the INCOMING lane — it shows what's about to land. Survive an attack clean to build meter.
  • A fake can look like any fruit — look for a faint purple shimmer. Slicing one costs points and 20% of your coins. Real strawberries pay big.
  • 90 seconds: SLICE → SABOTAGE → SURVIVE. Top score or KO wins.
