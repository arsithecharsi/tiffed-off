How to play

🔪 The Slicer swipes to slice fruit for points. Slicing a 💣 costs a heart — lose all 3 and the round ends. Missed fruit costs points.

😈 The Saboteur earns coins over time and spends them to attack: throw bombs into fruit clusters, send disguised trap fruit, fog the screen, flood it with chaos, or buy Eco for more income.

Each round lasts 90 seconds, then you swap roles. Highest slicing score wins!

Trap fruit look like watermelons… but they sparkle purple if you look closely. 👀
